DigiWallet Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: your cards stay on your iPhone. DigiWallet has no accounts and no ads, never tracks you, and never sends your cards, photos or details anywhere. The only thing that leaves your phone is a routine check for app updates.

What DigiWallet stores

When you add a card, DigiWallet saves on your iPhone:

  • The photos you take or choose of the front and back of the card.
  • The details read from those photos (for example member ID, group number or license number), and any details you type or edit.
  • Card names, folders and your settings, such as whether Face ID is on.
  • A scrambled (salted and hashed) version of your PIN, kept in the iOS Keychain. Your actual PIN is not stored.

All of this stays in DigiWallet's private storage on your device. The card list is also encrypted by the app.

How details are read

Text and barcodes are read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in on-device recognition. Your photos are never uploaded to be scanned.

What we collect

We never collect your cards, photos, card details, PIN or anything else you add. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites. The update check described below is the only data that leaves your device.

Network use

DigiWallet works fully offline. When a connection is available, it checks for and downloads updates to the app itself using Expo's update service (expo.dev). Each check sends:

  • A random install identifier created on your device. It isn't tied to your name, Apple ID or hardware.
  • The app version and device platform, so the right update is delivered.
  • If an update ever fails to start, the error message and the ID of the failed update, so it can be fixed. Expo deletes these after three days.

Like any internet request, the update service also receives your IP address. Expo stores the install identifier and IP address with each request. This information is used only to deliver updates and count how many devices received them. It is never linked to your identity and never used for advertising or tracking. Update checks never include your cards, photos, details, PIN or anything else you've added.

Camera, Photos and Face ID

  • Camera: used only when you scan a card.
  • Photos: DigiWallet uses Apple's private photo picker, so it can see only the photos you choose.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: checked by iOS. DigiWallet only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives your face or fingerprint data.

Sharing

A card leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself, for example with the Share button.

Backups

Like other apps, DigiWallet's data is included in your iPhone's own backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer. Those backups are handled by Apple under your device settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete any card in the app. Deleting DigiWallet from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device.

Children

DigiWallet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
