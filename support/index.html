DigiWallet Support

Need help? Send a support request and we'll get back to you. Please never include card numbers, IDs or photos of your cards.

Common questions

Where are my cards stored?
Only on your iPhone. DigiWallet has no account and no cloud. Your cards are never uploaded.

Some details weren't read correctly. Can I fix them?
Yes. Open the card and tap the pencil next to any detail, or tap Edit to change, add or remove details. For a driver's license, photograph the back too: DigiWallet reads the barcode for exact details.

The photo is crooked or sideways.
After taking or choosing a photo, tap Adjust Crop to set the corners, or use the rotate buttons. On a saved card, use Rotate next to Zoom, or ••• → Retake Photos.

I forgot my PIN.
On the lock screen tap Forgot PIN?. If Face ID or Touch ID is on, you can set a new PIN. Otherwise the only way back in is to erase DigiWallet's cards, because they are never stored anywhere else.

How do I move my cards to a new iPhone?
DigiWallet's data is included in your iPhone backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one.

Does DigiWallet work without internet?
Yes, completely. It only goes online to check for app updates.
